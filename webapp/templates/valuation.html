How to read this page

For each method, the target company's per-share fundamental (such as forward EPS) is multiplied by the selected peers' 25th percentile, median and 75th percentile valuation multiples. EV/EBITDA also subtracts target net debt and divides by shares outstanding. Methods that need positive denominators or unavailable balance-sheet inputs are omitted.

A positive difference means the peer multiple would imply a higher price than the current provider snapshot; it does not prove the stock is undervalued. Multiple choice, business quality, growth, accounting, capital structure, interest rates and data timing can explain the gap. The page intentionally does not label a stock “fair value” or produce a precise DCF target.
